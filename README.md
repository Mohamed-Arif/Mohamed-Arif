## Hi, I'm Arif 👋

I work in **procurement technology**, where operations meet automation. Most of my day is spent turning manual purchasing steps into reliable automated flows, such as taking purchase requisitions from intake through classification to purchase orders, and building AI agents that support procurement teams.

I like building practical tools that make repetitive work disappear, and I have a background in machine learning and data analysis.

### What I work with

- **Automation:** Power Automate, SharePoint and Excel-based workflows, AI agents
- **Languages:** Python, SQL, Java, JavaScript
- **Data and ML:** pandas, scikit-learn, PyTorch, Jupyter
- **Web:** Flask, HTML/CSS
